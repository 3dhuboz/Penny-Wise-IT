Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 April 2026

Penny Wise I.T ("we", "us") operates sales.pennywiseit.com.au. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Salesperson applicants: name, email, phone, location, and any "about you" text you submit via the apply form.
  • Active salespeople: everything above, plus the leads you add, your activity in the portal, your bank details (masked — only last 4 digits stored), your ABN.
  • Leads you add: business name, contact details, notes you enter.

How we use it

  • To run the sales portal and process commission payouts.
  • To send you transactional emails (scan results, stale-lead reminders, weekly digests, onboarding coaching).
  • To match leads across reps and prevent double-contacting.
  • To improve the AI lead scanner using aggregate patterns (we never share your individual leads with third parties).

Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare — our infrastructure provider (hosting, database).
  • Resend — to send transactional email on our behalf.
  • Serper.dev — when you run a Lead Scan, your search query (location + app type) is sent to their API.
  • We never sell your data to anyone.

Your rights (Australian Privacy Act)

  • You can request a copy of all data we hold about you by emailing steve@pennywiseit.com.au.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data.
  • You can unsubscribe from all marketing/coaching emails via the link at the bottom of any email.

Cookies / local storage

We use browser local storage for your session token (so you don't have to log in every time) and your last-used scan location. We don't use third-party tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions? Email steve@pennywiseit.com.au.
