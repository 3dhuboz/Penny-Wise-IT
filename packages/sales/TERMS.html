Terms of Engagement

For commission-only salespeople — Last updated 20 April 2026

1. Nature of the engagement

You are engaged as an independent contractor on a commission-only basis. You are not an employee. There is no guaranteed minimum income, no PAYG tax withholding, and no superannuation obligation on our end. You are responsible for your own tax, ABN, insurance, and any other business expenses.

2. What we pay

  • Setup commission: a % of the setup fee charged to the client (your rate is shown in the Commission tab of the portal).
  • Monthly recurring commission: a % of the monthly hosting/support fee the client pays each month, for as long as they remain a customer.
  • Commission is paid monthly by bank transfer after the client's funds have cleared.

3. What you do

  • Find prospective customers (using the Lead Scanner, cold outreach, referrals, or any lawful method).
  • Represent Penny Wise I.T professionally and honestly. Never make claims about our products that aren't true.
  • Mark deals as "Won" only once the client has formally agreed.
  • Hand off to our build team cleanly (all client contact info in the portal).

4. What we do

  • Build the product, host it, support it, invoice the client.
  • Provide you with the portal, AI tools, pitch scripts, and portfolio.
  • Pay your commission on time.

5. Conduct

  • You must not misrepresent our pricing, delivery timelines, or capabilities.
  • You must not poach clients from other reps (the dedup system prevents this anyway).
  • You must not use our brand, tools, or portfolio outside the purpose of selling Penny Wise I.T services.
  • Clients you bring in belong to Penny Wise I.T — you receive commission, not ownership.

6. Termination

Either party can end the engagement at any time, with written notice. After termination you continue to receive monthly recurring commission on any client who remains a paying customer, for as long as they stay. No setup commission will be paid on deals closed after termination.

7. Intellectual property

All Penny Wise I.T tools, templates, portfolio examples, and client materials are our property. You may use them while engaged but not afterwards. The portal's AI-generated outputs (follow-up messages, etc) are free for you to use with your clients.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Queensland, Australia.
