Getting started

I just got my login — what do I do first?

Log in, finish the 8-step onboarding (takes 5 minutes — the last step runs your first lead scan automatically). Then read the Sales Toolkit top-to-bottom — don't memorise, just see what's there. Finally, enable Daily Auto-Scan in Settings so the AI finds leads for you every hour while you're doing other things.

How much should I expect to earn?

Your commission is paid as a percentage of every setup fee plus every monthly hosting fee. Default rates are 15% setup + 10% monthly (your rates are visible in the Commission tab). 1 deal per week is the realistic minimum target. After year 1, your monthly recurring commission compounds — reps hitting 4 deals/month see $400-600/mo in recurring income just from previous months' work.

Do I need sales experience?

No. The Sales Toolkit has every pitch script, objection response, and message template you'll ever need. Our AI drafts personalised follow-ups. The Lead Scanner finds qualified prospects for you. You bring the conversations.

Lead Hunter

How do I find my first lead?

On the Today tab, type your area (e.g. "Brisbane") into the Lead Hunter, optionally pick an app type, and hit Find leads now. Results come back in 5–15 seconds. Look for the 🔥 hot badge — those are prospects actively asking for what you sell.

What do the confidence labels mean?

  • 🔥 Hot: They're explicitly asking for a website or app right now. Reply immediately.
  • ⚡ Warm: They have a pain point our product solves (e.g. complaining about Shopify). Worth reaching out.
  • ❄️ Cold: Mentioned the topic in passing. Use judgement.

What's the difference between Lead Scanner and Auto-Scan?

Lead Scanner is the manual "find me leads right now" tool. Auto-Scan runs the same search every hour automatically and drops new leads into your pipeline + emails you a daily digest. Set it once in Settings and forget.

Quoting

How do I send a quote?

Open the Quote Builder. Attach the quote to an existing lead (recommended — auto-advances their pipeline stage), or type a new business name. Pick an app type, toggle add-ons, and hit Email quote. The email is sent directly from your name at leads@pennywiseit.com.au. If you didn't attach a lead, one is auto-created at "Quote Sent" stage.

Can I save a reusable bundle?

Yes — build your quote, then hit + Save current as template. Next time, click the template pill to re-apply everything in one click.

Commission

When do I get paid?

Setup commission is paid the month after the client pays their setup fee. Monthly recurring compounds with every win — it's added to each month's payout. Your bank details live in Settings.

How do I know what's pending?

On the Today tab, the Earnings card shows your Pending payout (what's owed but not yet paid) and Recurring income.

Emails from Penny Wise I.T

What emails will I get?

  • Welcome email when you're added
  • Daily auto-scan digest (if enabled in Settings)
  • Stale lead reminders when leads haven't been touched in 5+ days
  • Coaching nudges in your first 14 days
  • Friday weekly wrap with your stats + leaderboard rank
  • Sunday planning note with a week-ahead focus

How do I stop getting these emails?

Every email has an Unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and we'll stop. You can resubscribe from Settings any time. Your in-app messages from Steve still work regardless.

Troubleshooting

My scan came back with 0 leads

Try a broader location (e.g. "Queensland" instead of a suburb) or leave the app type on "All types". Low-volume areas sometimes have quiet days — check back in a few hours. If persistent, message Steve via the 🔔 From Steve tab.

The scanner keeps showing competitors

The AI filters these aggressively but some slip through. Just click Dismiss on any competitor you see — it won't come back. If a specific source is particularly noisy, flag it to Steve.

I can't log in

Hit "Forgot password" or email steve@pennywiseit.com.au with your username. Most issues clear up with a password reset.

My email quote isn't sending

Make sure the client's email is filled in on the Quote Builder form. If Resend fails, it'll fall back to opening your local email app (Outlook / Apple Mail).

Still stuck? Use the 🔔 From Steve tab in the portal and Steve will reply within 24 hours.
